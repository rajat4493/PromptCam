# Intent

## What was asked for, in the founder's terms

> Apple has announced iPhone Duo, a foldable iPhone with inner and outer
> displays. I want to move quickly and capture an early App Store advantage
> with an experience that is genuinely useful *because of* the new hardware.
>
> PromptCam turns one iPhone Duo into both a camera and director console for
> the person recording, and a prompt and preview screen for the person being
> recorded.

The canonical scenario: a creator interviews someone using the rear cameras.
The creator sees camera controls, upcoming questions and recording status on the
inner display. The interviewee sees the current question, a countdown and
optionally their own preview on the outer display facing them.

It must feel designed for iPhone Duo, not an ordinary iPhone interface
stretched onto a larger screen — while remaining useful on an ordinary iPhone.

## The promise

**Turn one iPhone into a camera operator and an interview producer.**

## Why this is a real problem

One person cannot simultaneously operate a camera and produce an interview.
A solo creator is holding framing, watching audio, remembering the question
list, deciding when to move on, and marking the moments worth keeping. Something
always drops. The failure modes are specific:

- the interviewee forgets the question mid-answer, wasting the take;
- the creator reads questions off a second device, breaking eye contact;
- the interviewee does not know when recording started, so the first seconds
  are a confused pause;
- good moments go unmarked, so the editor re-watches forty minutes for six
  usable clips.

None of these are camera-quality problems. They are production problems, and
they are what makes a one-person shoot look amateur.

## Target user

A solo creator or two-person team recording **other people** talking, on a
phone, without a crew: street interviews, customer testimonials, podcast clips,
founder interviews, employee and event interviews, short-form Q&A.

Not the V0 user: broadcast crews (they have hardware prompters and monitors),
people recording themselves (a teleprompter is a different product, explicitly
excluded), and anyone whose problem is editing rather than capture.

## The wedge

**The only app where the person being interviewed can read the question
themselves.**

Not "a camera app with more features". The wedge is the subject-facing surface;
everything else in V0 exists to make it useful — decks give it content, the
state machine keeps it truthful, markers make the footage editable.

Defensibility in the short term is execution and timing, not technology. Stated
plainly: this is a land-grab on a new hardware affordance, and the moat is being
early with something that actually works.

## What "done" was defined as

> The smallest credible PromptCam that can be tested honestly, submitted
> quickly, and expanded safely after real user evidence.

Not an app-shaped codebase. The operative word is **honestly** — which is why
the verification ledger, not the feature list, decides whether a milestone
closes.
